**Borealis** 1.3.0 turns the Play tab into a full interactive sheet with dice rolls, lets a character keep several companions, adds A4 paper for the character sheet, and fixes the bugs reported on the Borealis Discord and in our first tester sessions. It also includes everything from 1.2.2, which was only released to the app stores.

**Download for your platform at [borealisbuilder.com](https://borealisbuilder.com)**, or pick a file below.

**The macOS app is now signed and notarised by Apple**: it opens with a double-click, with no right-click or Privacy & Security step (#17).

The App Store and Google Play versions are still going through review; they will move to 1.3.0 once they are through.

Installing 1.3.0 over 1.2.1, 1.2.0, 1.1.x or 1.0.x keeps your characters, content and settings.

**If you use Borealis on more than one device, update all of them.** Borealis 1.2.0 and older drop data they don't know about (spell slot use, concentration, custom trackers, and now companions and Play-tab state) when they save a character. From 1.2.1 on, an older app keeps data written by a newer one.

### New in 1.3.0
- **The Play tab is a full interactive sheet** (#14): abilities, saving throws, skills, attacks and spells with their descriptions, ready to use at the table.
- **Dice rolls on the Play tab** (#1). Roll buttons on abilities, saves, skills, attacks and spells, a roll menu for advantage and disadvantage, a result card and your recent rolls.
- **Several companions** (#13). The Companion tab shows for every character and can hold any number of companions or pets, each with its own traits and bonuses.
- **Rolled or average hit points for each level** (#16), chosen in the level menu.
- **Choose how you generate ability scores** (#12) on Build > Ability Scores.
- **A4 paper for the character sheet.** Settings > Character sheet has a Paper setting, Letter or A4. Until you pick one it follows your region: Letter in the US, Canada, Mexico, the Philippines and the other Letter countries, A4 elsewhere.
- **Weapon Mastery explains itself.** Each mastery choice says what you are picking, each weapon's card shows its mastery and what it does, and on phones every row shows the weapon's mastery.
- **On phones, the rules-version sheet** shows a short description of each version, so two versions of the same option can be compared.
- **Each imported book has an edition**: 2014, 2024 or Both editions. A Both editions book is offered to 2014 and 2024 characters alike. Borealis guesses the edition when you import: 2024 only when the book says so (its 5.5e mark, or its name or description saying 2024, 5.5e or One D&D), Both editions when it names both, and 2014 otherwise. Official books released on or after 17 September 2024 count as 2024. You can change any book's edition in the import preview or later on the Sources page; your choice is kept when you re-import the book.
- **Help > Privacy Policy** opens the Borealis privacy page in your browser.
- **5etools-format import wording.** The import screens describe the data format and the kinds of links that work, and imported books show as "5etools-format: *book name*".

### Fixed in 1.3.0
- **Spell filters** (#21) apply to the class whose spells you are choosing, also for single-class casters.
- **Default sources** (#22): the sources you save as default now apply when Borealis starts and to every new character.
- **Sub races from imported content** (#23). Tiefling sub races, and Human and Half-Orc variants, from imported books now appear on the built-in races, even when the core race file isn't imported.
- **Character portrait** (#18, #19). Clicking the portrait opens the portrait gallery, on phones too.
- **Phones: item management** (#20). The equipment action bars stay pinned at the bottom of the screen while you scroll the item list.
- **Next selection** asks for the choice in front of you (for example Draconic Ancestry before Class) and scrolls to it; switching to another tab starts that tab at the top.
- **Character sheet preview**: page 1 no longer overlaps its HP, hit dice and death saves boxes, and Size and Speed fit. On phones, Generate Preview opens the sheet full-screen with Save, Share and Close; Share lets you print.
- **Rage**: switching Rage on uses one Rage, and it can't be switched on with none left. The same goes for any state that has its own uses.
- **Phones: the status line** no longer squeezes into a narrow column while a command runs.
- **Weapon Mastery**: a weapon chosen in one slot is no longer offered in the other, and the slots count as required choices.
- **Magic Initiate** spells can be chosen without a spellcasting class, and its spellcasting ability is asked on the Feats tab, before its spells.
- **Help links** (#9). News, Releases, Support Page and the globe in the title bar open their pages in your browser.
- **Empty Class list** (#10). A character gets only the classes of its own edition; an empty Class list now says why and offers **Start over as 2014**, which makes a 2014 copy and leaves the original untouched.
- **Homebrew imports** (#11). No more wall of warnings above every Build page: a content problem shows only where your character meets it, and the import report still lists every problem. Choices whose tags contain colons, apostrophes or letters such as ō offer their options, and 2024 content that replaces 2014 content still counts where the 2014 version is asked for.
- **Android phones and tablets.** Opening a dialog no longer brings up the keyboard before you tap a field; on tablets the title bar clears the system bars and has no window buttons that did nothing.
