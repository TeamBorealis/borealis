**Borealis** 1.4.0 adds house rules to ignore feat and multiclass prerequisites, reworks attack editing, gives the inventory Aurora's item menu, and fixes the bugs reported on the Borealis Discord.

**Download for your platform at [borealisbuilder.com](https://borealisbuilder.com)**, or pick a file below.

| Platform | File |
|---|---|
| macOS (Apple silicon, macOS 10.15 or later) | `Borealis_1.4.0_aarch64.dmg` |
| Windows (x64) | `Borealis_1.4.0_x64-setup.exe` |
| Linux (x86_64), any distribution | `Borealis_1.4.0_amd64.AppImage` |
| Debian / Ubuntu | `Borealis_1.4.0_amd64.deb` |
| Fedora / openSUSE | `Borealis-1.4.0-1.x86_64.rpm` |
| Android (64-bit ARM), sideload | `Borealis_1.4.0_arm64.apk` |

Installing 1.4.0 over any earlier version keeps your characters, content and settings.

### New in 1.4.0
- **Ignore feat and multiclass prerequisites** (#24). Two new house rules under Edition & compatibility let a character take a feat or a multiclass without meeting its prerequisites. Such picks are marked "prerequisites not met", and turning a rule off keeps them, marked invalid.
- **Custom attacks can follow an ability** (#29). Tick "Calculate automatically", pick the ability, and the attack bonus and damage follow it like a weapon's.
- **The inventory has Aurora's item menu** (#28). Right-click an item, or long-press on phones, for the item menu, including "Add to Attacks & Spellcasting" for weapons.
- **Gear on phones: the inventory is its own sub-tab** (#33), beside Equipment and Additional treasure, and Gear opens on it once you carry items, so there is no more scrolling past the whole item list to reach it.
- **One settings window** (#31). Application Settings now holds every setting: the Borealis settings that had their own window are tabs in it (Rules, Saving, Display, Content, Search, AI assistant, Phones, Devices, Updates), saved with Save and undone with Cancel like the others.

### Fixed in 1.4.0
- **Editing a weapon attack saves** (#27). Pick another ability and the attack bonus and damage are worked out again; renaming it, changing its texts and leaving it off the sheet are kept too.
- **Dragonborn resistance** (#26). Defenses on the character sheet, the printed sheet and the Play tab list only the resistance of the chosen Draconic Ancestry, not every ancestry's.
- **Manage > Character takes typed text** (#30). Gender, Deity, Eyes, Skin, Hair and the Backstory Trinket accept anything you type as well as a pick from the list.
- **Unavailable folders keep your content.** If your secondary content folder or custom documents folder is on a drive or share that isn't connected, Borealis keeps your imported content and tells you the folder is unavailable, instead of removing the content or quietly switching folders.
