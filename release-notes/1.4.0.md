**Borealis** 1.4.0 tracks hit points and hit dice on the Play tab, puts every setting in one window, learns to update itself, adds house rules to ignore feat and multiclass prerequisites, reworks attack editing and fixes the bugs reported on the Borealis Discord.

**Download for your platform at [borealisbuilder.com](https://borealisbuilder.com)**, or pick a file below.

| Platform | File |
|---|---|
| macOS (Apple silicon, macOS 10.15 or later) | `Borealis_1.4.0_aarch64.dmg` |
| Windows (x64) | `Borealis_1.4.0_x64-setup.exe` |
| Linux (x86_64), any distribution | `Borealis_1.4.0_amd64.AppImage` |
| Debian / Ubuntu | `Borealis_1.4.0_amd64.deb` |
| Fedora / openSUSE | `Borealis-1.4.0-1.x86_64.rpm` |
| Android (64-bit ARM), sideload | `Borealis_1.4.0_arm64.apk` |

Installing 1.4.0 over any earlier version keeps your characters, content and settings.

### New in 1.4.0
- **Borealis opens much faster when you have imported a lot of content.** Loading no longer slows down with every imported book, and the loaded content is kept ready for the next launch. A large imported library that took over 10 seconds to load now takes about a second or less.
- **Ignore feat and multiclass prerequisites** (#24). Two new house rules under Edition & compatibility let a character take a feat or a multiclass without meeting its prerequisites. Such picks are marked "prerequisites not met", and turning a rule off keeps them, marked invalid.
- **Custom attacks can follow an ability** (#29). Tick "Calculate automatically", pick the ability, and the attack bonus and damage follow it like a weapon's.
- **The inventory has Aurora's item menu** (#28). Right-click an item, or long-press on phones, for the item menu, including "Add to Attacks & Spellcasting" for weapons.
- **Gear on phones: the inventory is its own sub-tab** (#33), beside Equipment and Additional treasure, and Gear opens on it once you carry items, so there is no more scrolling past the whole item list to reach it.
- **One settings window** (#31). Application Settings now holds every setting: the Borealis settings that had their own window are tabs in it (Rules, Saving, Display, Content, Search, AI assistant, Phones, Devices, Updates), saved with Save and undone with Cancel like the others.
- **Borealis can update itself** (#25) on Windows, macOS and the Linux AppImage. From the next release on, the update notice has an **Install and restart** button next to Download, so there is no need to download each new version from the website. Nothing installs until you click it. The deb and rpm packages and the store versions update as before.
- **Play: hit points, hit dice and amounts.** Track current and temporary hit points on the Play tab (damage comes off temporary hit points first), spend hit dice to heal and get them back on a Long Rest by your edition's rules (a Short Rest offers them), and change any tracker or your hit points by a typed amount.
- **Portrait library** (#32). Settings > Content has a Portrait library folder. Its images (PNG, JPEG, GIF and WebP, also one sub-folder down) show in the portrait gallery after your own, and picking one sets the portrait as browsing for an image does.
- **Play: switch Rage on its own row.** A state that has uses, such as Rage, now has its switch on its row under Resources, next to Spend, so spending a use and switching it on happen in one place.
- **Obsidian vault notes** (#15). Settings > Content has a new Obsidian vault folder, and Start > Notes shows its notes. Read them as rendered pages (properties, links, embeds, tags, callouts, task lists) or edit their Markdown as is. Follow links, see backlinks, search by text or tag, and create, rename (with link updates if you agree) and delete notes. Borealis works on the files in place and never changes your `.obsidian` settings. If Obsidian or a sync service changes a note while you edit it, Borealis asks before saving. Desktop only for now.
- **Add the extras your DM grants** (#35). Feats, Languages and Proficiencies on Build, and each spellcasting class on Magic, have an Add button for a feat, language, proficiency or spell your DM gives you. Added picks are marked "Added", can be removed and don't clutter your equipment. With "Ignore feat prerequisites" on, every installed option is offered.
- **Split a higher starting level between classes** (#34). In Level up & multiclassing, Move levels gives some of your class's levels to a new multiclass, so a level 9 Fighter becomes Fighter 5 / Wizard 4 without levelling up one level at a time. Multiclass prerequisites apply as usual, and Remove gives the levels back.
- **Guides in the app.** A small help button beside New Character, the Aurora and 5etools-format imports, Send/Receive to device and the character sheet's Save opens the matching guide on borealisbuilder.com.
- **A Long Rest at 0 hit points asks first.** By the rules a long rest gives no benefit at 0 hit points; you can still rest anyway.

### Fixed in 1.4.0
- **Editing a weapon attack saves** (#27). Pick another ability and the attack bonus and damage are worked out again; renaming it, changing its texts and leaving it off the sheet are kept too.
- **Dragonborn resistance** (#26). Defenses on the character sheet, the printed sheet and the Play tab list only the resistance of the chosen Draconic Ancestry, not every ancestry's.
- **Manage > Character takes typed text** (#30). Gender, Deity, Eyes, Skin, Hair and the Backstory Trinket accept anything you type as well as a pick from the list.
- **Manage > Character alignment and deity reach the sheet.** Alignment and Deity are now the character's alignment and deity choices, as in the builder: picking one selects it, the sheet prints it, an imported Aurora character shows its alignment in Manage, and a deity you type yourself is kept and printed.
- **The printed sheet marks picks that ignore prerequisites.** A feat or multiclass taken with "Ignore feat prerequisites" or "Ignore multiclass prerequisites" is printed with an asterisk, and the page footer explains it.
- **Unavailable folders keep your content.** If your secondary content folder or custom documents folder is on a drive or share that isn't connected, Borealis keeps your imported content and tells you the folder is unavailable, instead of removing the content or quietly switching folders.
- **Additional treasure and quest items reach the sheet.** Text you type for them is printed on the character sheet, and an imported Aurora character shows its treasure and quest items in the app.
- **Plain wording on converted species.** A 2014 species used in a 2024 character now says which ability score increases are not applied (for example "Strength +2, Constitution +1") instead of showing an internal setting name.
- **Long item descriptions no longer push the Equipment page off screen.** The description scrolls in its own pane.
- **2014 Bards always have Bardic Inspiration.** A 2014 Bard with Charisma 11 or lower now has one use of Bardic Inspiration instead of none.
- **A long spell description no longer stretches the Magic page.** It scrolls inside the description pane, as on the Equipment page.
- **The filter count fits on phones.** Pickers show "9/11" instead of a cut-off "9 of …", and screen readers still hear "9 of 11 entries".
- **Restore Defaults keeps your folders.** The documents folder, the secondary content folder, the portrait library and the Obsidian vault stay as you chose them.
- **Non-binary is in the Gender list on the Manage page**, as it already was in New Character.
- **Spells added through an "Additional Spell" item use your character's edition.** A 2024 character got the 2014 version of the spell.
