**Borealis** 1.4.1 imports single 5etools-format homebrew files and fixes the bugs reported on the Borealis Discord since 1.4.0.

**Download for your platform at [borealisbuilder.com](https://borealisbuilder.com)**, or pick a file below.

| Platform | File |
|---|---|
| macOS (Apple silicon, macOS 10.15 or later) | `Borealis_1.4.1_aarch64.dmg` |
| Windows (x64) | `Borealis_1.4.1_x64-setup.exe` |
| Linux (x86_64), any distribution | `Borealis_1.4.1_amd64.AppImage` |
| Debian / Ubuntu | `Borealis_1.4.1_amd64.deb` |
| Fedora / openSUSE | `Borealis-1.4.1-1.x86_64.rpm` |
| Android (64-bit ARM), sideload | `Borealis_1.4.1_arm64.apk` |

Installing 1.4.1 over any earlier version keeps your characters, content and settings. On Windows, macOS and the Linux AppImage, **Install and restart** in the update notice installs it for you.

### New in 1.4.1
- **Import a single 5etools-format homebrew file.** Additional Content > 5etools-format import has a new **Choose 5etools-format homebrew .json files** button on desktop and phones: pick one homebrew file (or several at once, optionally with a .zip of your data/ folder), and its spells, items, creatures, classes, subclasses, species, feats, backgrounds and more import as a pack named after the file's own source. The source list shows what the file holds that Borealis does not import (deities, vehicles, tables...) and which other homebrew files it needs. A homebrew file chosen under Custom content, dropped on the window or opened with Borealis now goes to the 5etools-format import instead of failing with "unknown field _meta".
- **Play marks prepared spells.** Play > Spells shows a dot beside each prepared spell and a diamond beside each spell that is always prepared, as the character sheet does, so you can see which spells come free from a feature.
- **The companion view shows saving throws and senses.** The companion stats view has a Saving Throws section, and lists the companion's senses (such as Darkvision), languages and damage and condition resistances and immunities, as the character sheet's companion page does.

### Fixed in 1.4.1
- **See which content an imported character is missing** (#37). When an imported character names content that is not installed, the notice on the Build page now has a **Show missing content** link. It lists every missing element by name, type and id, grouped by the source its id names, and you can add that content and re-check from there. **Re-check missing content** on the Characters page now lists what is still missing as well, so you no longer get only a count.
- **Spells a subclass always gives you are marked on the sheet** (#39). With imported Aurora content, spells a class or subclass feature grants, such as a Bard college's or a Warlock patron's spells, now show the "always prepared" diamond on the character sheet's spell table, not the dot for a spell you learned. The Magic tab also shows them as always prepared, so you can see at a glance which spells already come from a feature and don't spend a new spell on one.
- **Spells your species or a class feature grants show on the sheet and in Play** (#38). In imported Aurora content, spells granted without a spellcasting class, such as an invocation's Mage Armor, a Drow's Dancing Lights and Faerie Fire or a subclass's rituals, now print in an "Other Spells" section after your spellcasting classes, each named with where it came from, as Aurora prints them. They get spell cards, and Play lists them under Other Spells without spending a spell slot. Spells a feat such as Magic Initiate teaches also go there when the content names no spellcasting ability for them, named after their spell list as in Aurora (e.g. "True Strike (Wizard)"), instead of a block of their own with blank save DC and attack boxes; a feat whose ability is known keeps its own block. Spells a feat teaches before you choose its spellcasting ability show there too, and the PDF sheet leaves the ability, save DC and attack boxes blank for a block with no spellcasting ability instead of printing +0.
- **Magic: a feat's spells have a tab of their own.** With a spellcasting class and a feat that grants spells, such as Magic Initiate, the feat's tab on the Magic page showed the class's spells, and a feat's spell selection could open its choices inside the class's block. The feat's tab now shows the feat's own spells, and ticking a spell there no longer changes the class's prepared spells. Item and subclass spell blocks get the same fix.
- **Companion pages show saving throws and senses** (#40). The companion page of the character sheet now lists the companion's saving throws, its special senses such as Darkvision, its languages and its damage and condition resistances and immunities, as Aurora prints them. A chosen companion no longer reports "refers to unknown group 'companion:proficiency'" content notices, and its skill and save proficiencies now count. A familiar's actions stay listed on its page, as in Aurora, even though a familiar can't attack.
