**Borealis** 1.4.1 imports single 5etools-format homebrew files and fixes the bugs reported on the Borealis Discord since 1.4.0.

**Download for your platform at [borealisbuilder.com](https://borealisbuilder.com)**, or pick a file below.

| Platform | File |
|---|---|
| macOS (Apple silicon, macOS 10.15 or later) | `Borealis_1.4.1_aarch64.dmg` |
| Windows (x64) | `Borealis_1.4.1_x64-setup.exe` |
| Linux (x86_64), any distribution | `Borealis_1.4.1_amd64.AppImage` |
| Debian / Ubuntu | `Borealis_1.4.1_amd64.deb` |
| Fedora / openSUSE | `Borealis-1.4.1-1.x86_64.rpm` |
| Android (64-bit ARM), sideload | `Borealis_1.4.1_arm64.apk` |

Installing 1.4.1 over any earlier version keeps your characters, content and settings. On Windows, macOS and the Linux AppImage, **Install and restart** in the update notice installs it for you.

### New in 1.4.1
- **Import a single 5etools-format homebrew file.** Additional Content > 5etools-format import has a new **Choose 5etools-format homebrew .json files** button on desktop and phones: pick one homebrew file (or several at once, optionally with a .zip of your data/ folder), and its spells, items, creatures, classes, subclasses, species, feats, backgrounds and more import as a pack named after the file's own source. The source list shows what the file holds that Borealis does not import (deities, vehicles, tables...) and which other homebrew files it needs. A homebrew file chosen under Custom content, dropped on the window or opened with Borealis now goes to the 5etools-format import instead of failing with "unknown field _meta".
- **Play marks prepared spells.** Play > Spells shows a dot beside each prepared spell and a diamond beside each spell that is always prepared, as the character sheet does, so you can see which spells come free from a feature.
- **The companion view shows saving throws and senses.** The companion stats view has a Saving Throws section, and lists the companion's senses (such as Darkvision), languages and damage and condition resistances and immunities, as the character sheet's companion page does.
- **A weapon can have more than one mastery property.** Homebrew weapons that list several, in Borealis, Aurora or 5etools-format content, now show every property with its rules on the Weapon Mastery pick, the attack and the character sheet. Aurora-format weapons that give their mastery the way Aurora content does now get it, where before they had none.

### Easier character building (from tester sessions)
- **Phones: tap a row's circle to choose it.** In the builder's lists the circle at the end of each row now chooses that entry; tapping the name still opens its description, and the Choose button and double tap still work.
- **Phones: descriptions open right under the row you tap,** instead of below every list on the page. Tap the row again to close it.
- **Descriptions read as text, not data.** The card no longer shows Type, Category and a second Source line, and a species or class now shows what each of its traits and features does instead of only their names.
- **Class cards show level 1 first.** The Grants list shows what a class gives at level 1 and folds the later levels under "Later levels"; the multiclass proficiencies no longer appear as duplicates.
- **Background cards list everything they give,** including the feat (for example Magic Initiate (Cleric)), the ability score choice and the starting equipment, and no longer print their summary twice.
- **Edition filters start on your character's edition** in every picker and in the equipment list, and a change carries from list to list; Clear goes back to your character's edition.
- **No more dead-end edition filter:** a list that holds only one edition (a 2024 character's classes, for example) no longer offers the other one.
- **Phones: no more "SRD 5.2.1" on every row** when the whole list comes from one source; mixed lists keep it.
- **The Species picker says Species** (and "Search species") for a 2024 character, matching its tab.
- **Build no longer flashes "Race" and a Loading card** when you come back to it.
- **Play: each resource says how its uses come back.** Rage reads "1 back on a Short Rest, all on a Long Rest" and Breath Weapon "All back on a Long Rest", in place of the grey label that repeated the name. The details no longer claim a Short Rest resets Rage.
- **Short Rest and Long Rest say what they did.** A message names what came back ("Short Rest: Rage +1.") or says there was nothing to regain, so a rest is never silent.
- **Phones: a finished character leads to its sheet.** When you make your last selection, the status bar on Build, Magic and Gear offers View sheet and Play where Next selection stood. It goes away when you tap one, dismiss it, or leave those tabs, and a character you open already finished keeps a clean status line.
- **Phones: Save Character Sheet asks where to save.** The system save picker opens, the message says where the PDF went ("Saved to Downloads/Sable_Marrow.pdf"), and on Android the sheet then opens in your PDF viewer, where you can print it. Share in the preview still sends it to other apps.
- **Phones: each tab counts its own open choices.** The badge on Build no longer counts spell picks that are made on Magic; Magic shows its own count, and Gear counts starting equipment you have not chosen yet.
- **Phones: the keyboard gets the room.** While the on-screen keyboard is open, the status line, Next selection and the tab bar step aside so they no longer cover the list or the field you type in; they come back when it closes.
- **Phones: the status line says what to do next and where,** for example "Next: choose Magic Initiate Cantrips, 1 of 2 (Magic tab)", instead of "Choose one from the 'Magic Initiate Cantrips' selection option.".
- **Starting equipment where you equip.** On phones the class and background starting-equipment choices now sit at the top of Gear > Equipment instead of under Additional treasure, and each has an **I'll buy my own** button for buying your gear item by item. Until you choose or decline it, it counts as an open choice on the Gear tab and Next selection takes you there.
- **The Feats tab shows the feats your build already has,** such as Magic Initiate (Cleric) from the Acolyte background, instead of saying there is nothing there.
- **Background ability-score options explain themselves.** Choosing an option such as "Intelligence +1, Charisma +2" shows each score and modifier it changes, from what to what; and on phones the Choose button no longer shifts the page when it appears or goes.
- **Ability Scores suggest scores for your class.** For a 2024 class the tab shows "Suggested for Barbarian" (that class's standard array) with an Apply button; nothing changes until you press it.

### Fixed in 1.4.1
- **See which content an imported character is missing** (#37). When an imported character names content that is not installed, the notice on the Build page now has a **Show missing content** link. It lists every missing element by name, type and id, grouped by the source its id names, and you can add that content and re-check from there. **Re-check missing content** on the Characters page now lists what is still missing as well, so you no longer get only a count.
- **Spells a subclass always gives you are marked on the sheet** (#39). With imported Aurora content, spells a class or subclass feature grants, such as a Bard college's or a Warlock patron's spells, now show the "always prepared" diamond on the character sheet's spell table, not the dot for a spell you learned. The Magic tab also shows them as always prepared, so you can see at a glance which spells already come from a feature and don't spend a new spell on one.
- **Spells your species or a class feature grants show on the sheet and in Play** (#38). In imported Aurora content, spells granted without a spellcasting class, such as an invocation's Mage Armor, a Drow's Dancing Lights and Faerie Fire or a subclass's rituals, now print in an "Other Spells" section after your spellcasting classes, each named with where it came from, as Aurora prints them. They get spell cards, and Play lists them under Other Spells without spending a spell slot. Spells a feat such as Magic Initiate teaches also go there when the content names no spellcasting ability for them, named after their spell list as in Aurora (e.g. "True Strike (Wizard)"), instead of a block of their own with blank save DC and attack boxes; a feat whose ability is known keeps its own block. Spells a feat teaches before you choose its spellcasting ability show there too, and the PDF sheet leaves the ability, save DC and attack boxes blank for a block with no spellcasting ability instead of printing +0.
- **Magic: a feat's spells have a tab of their own.** With a spellcasting class and a feat that grants spells, such as Magic Initiate, the feat's tab on the Magic page showed the class's spells, and a feat's spell selection could open its choices inside the class's block. The feat's tab now shows the feat's own spells, and ticking a spell there no longer changes the class's prepared spells. Item and subclass spell blocks get the same fix.
- **Companion pages show saving throws and senses** (#40). The companion page of the character sheet now lists the companion's saving throws, its special senses such as Darkvision, its languages and its damage and condition resistances and immunities, as Aurora prints them. A chosen companion no longer reports "refers to unknown group 'companion:proficiency'" content notices, and its skill and save proficiencies now count. A familiar's actions stay listed on its page, as in Aurora, even though a familiar can't attack.
- **Sheet preview: every 2014 page is the size of the paper.** Page 1 and the spellcasting pages are now split the way the printed sheet splits them, so the preview pages match the PDF; text that does not fit continues on a later page, marked "continued on page N". The printed 2014 sheet's "+N more" features line now points at the page those features are on.
- **Phones: dropdowns open as a list sheet.** Every dropdown on a phone, such as Gear's Category, now opens a list from the bottom of the screen with large rows and the current choice ticked, instead of the system dialog that could pick the row next to the one you tapped. Swipe it down to cancel.
- **Spells given to a class written in lower case now count for that class.** In imported Aurora content, spells a feature gives a class whose name is written in lower case (for example a Paladin oath's spells written as `paladin`) now appear in that class's spellcasting section with its save DC and attack bonus, and are always prepared where the content says so, as Aurora shows them. Before, they were listed under Other Spells with blank boxes.
- **Gear: categories of the other edition list their items.** With the Edition filter on your character's edition, a category whose items are all from the other edition, such as Trade Goods for a 2024 character, showed a count (Trade Goods (23)) but an empty list. It now lists them, and the Edition filter shows only in categories that hold both editions.
- **iPhone: Share no longer adds a text file.** Sharing the character sheet from the preview, or a Borealis bundle, and choosing Save to Files saved a small "text.txt" next to the file. Now only the file is shared.
- **iPhone: the Save message says when Files may have renamed the sheet.** When a sheet of that name was already in the folder and you chose Keep Both, the message named only the folder. It now says the Files app may have given the new copy another name, for example "Saved to On My iPhone (Files may have renamed it, as Sable_Marrow.pdf was already there)".
