**Borealis** 1.5.0 brings the Homebrew Creator: make your own spells, feats, classes, species, backgrounds, items, creatures and more inside Borealis, with forms and plain sentences, try them on a test character and share them as one file.

**Download for your platform at [borealisbuilder.com](https://borealisbuilder.com)**, or pick a file below.

| Platform | File |
|---|---|
| macOS (Apple silicon, macOS 10.15 or later) | `Borealis_1.5.0_aarch64.dmg` |
| Windows (x64) | `Borealis_1.5.0_x64-setup.exe` |
| Linux (x86_64), any distribution | `Borealis_1.5.0_amd64.AppImage` |
| Debian / Ubuntu | `Borealis_1.5.0_amd64.deb` |

Installing 1.5.0 over any earlier version keeps your characters, content and settings. On Windows, macOS and the Linux AppImage, **Install and restart** in the update notice installs it for you.

### New in 1.5.0: the Homebrew Creator
- **Start > Homebrew** holds your own packs. Make a pack with a name, an edition (2014, 2024 or both) and a version, and open it: the kinds of element with their counts, the element list, the editor and the element's live description, laid out like the Equipment page. What you save loads like any other content, so your characters can take it straight away.
- **Every kind of content.** Spells, feats, species and 2014 races with their subraces and traits, classes, subclasses, class options such as fighting styles, backgrounds, creatures (full stat blocks for companions, familiars and wild shapes), items, weapons, armor, magic items and starting equipment, and the smaller lists such as languages, conditions, skills and deities. **Other element** makes anything else, for experts.
- **Forms, not files.** Every field has its label and, where needed, a line of help. Anything that names other content is picked from a list, and conditions such as "only while wearing no armor" are built from choices, so there is never an id, a formula or an escaped character to type. Descriptions have bold, italic, headings, lists and tables.
- **Rules as plain sentences.** A rule gives something, lets the player choose, or changes a number, and ready-made rules cover common features: a pool of uses, extra damage, a sense, advantage on a save and many more.
- **Classes from level 1 to 20.** The Levels grid shows each level's features; add one with the + on its row. **Add spellcasting** gives a class its ability, spell list and slots (full, half, third, pact magic or your own table), and multiclassing and starting equipment have their own tabs.
- **Try it.** Test any element on a character built in memory: change the edition, level and ability scores, make its choices and read its hit points, Armor Class, skills, features and spell slots. The level ladder shows what is gained at every level from 1 to 20. The test character is never saved.
- **Problems as you type.** Borealis checks the element while you work and says what is wrong in plain words ("A spell needs a school."); click a problem to jump to its field. It also tries the element on a character at every level from 1 to 20 before you save.
- **Nothing is lost.** Every change is kept on your device as you type, through a crash or a restart. An element you changed is marked in the list and asks **Keep editing** or **Discard** when you open it again, and a new element you never saved stays listed as "Not saved yet". Undo and Redo (Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Shift+Z) cover every change.
- **Start from a copy** of a System Reference Document element or of anything installed, a whole class included. **Copy to homebrew** in the Compendium and the builder does the same, and **Edit** on installed content makes a copy, so updates never undo your changes. **Spell Lists** adds your spells to installed classes' lists.
- **Share a pack.** **Export** puts a pack in one file anyone with Borealis can import and edit, on any platform. Text you copied from content you imported is left out unless you choose otherwise. **Import** shows the usual preview first and lets you replace your pack (it asks first when characters on this device use something the file doesn't have) or add the file as a copy, and **Send** moves packs to your other devices on the same network.
- **For experts:** a read-only Data view of each element, Where used, multi-select edits (edition, tags, move, duplicate) undone in one step, and a keyboard shortcut for everything (**Keys** lists them).
- **On phones too.** The whole creator works on a phone: one screen at a time, Undo, Redo, Try it and Save pinned at the bottom, pickers and dialogs as sheets, the formatting buttons above the keyboard and tables edited cell by cell, bold and italic included.
- **A guide for it.** The help button in New pack and New element opens the new guide, [Make your own homebrew](https://borealisbuilder.com/guides/homebrew-creator).

### More in the Homebrew Creator
- **Suggestions from your content.** An item's Category offers the categories your installed items use, Tags suggests the tags installed elements of the same kind carry, and the Spell list of a spellcasting section suggests the lists your installed classes have (and still takes a new one).
- **Spell lists.** A spell's Lists also offers the lists of classes that write their spell list as a combination (such as "Cleric or Wizard"). A list that no installed class or choice uses gets a warning that names the near spelling ("there is a list Wizard"), and a spell on no list gets a hint. On phones, **Select** chooses spells with a tap for Add selected and Remove selected, where a desktop uses Ctrl+click and Shift+click.
- **Your own skills.** A skill you make is a real skill on every character: its own row with its ability's modifier and passive score, on the sheet and the PDF. A background, a class's skill choice or any "choose a skill" can give proficiency in it, and expertise works as for the others. The **Bonus to skills** and **Advantage on a roll** rules offer your skills after the eighteen standard ones; the bonus shows on the skill's row and the advantage is noted with the character's advantages, in Try it too.
- **Classes.** **Insert class table** in the description adds the class table from the Levels grid: level, proficiency bonus, features and the class's own columns, and for a 2014 spellcaster its cantrips known, spells known and spell slots. A class copied with its subclasses names them in the editor ("Saved with it: ...") before you save.
- **Class options.** A warning when no installed class's choice offers the option's group, with the near spelling when there is one.
- **2014 races.** A Subraces field says whether the player chooses one of the race's subraces, instead of a rule block.
- **Backgrounds.** Variants that a background offers by tag (as many imported backgrounds do) are listed in its Variants field, editing that list keeps offering the tag's variants from other packs, and the origin feat picker offers origin feats only.
- **Feats.** A prerequisite on a statistic reads in words, such as "A flying speed" or "Darkvision range 60+".
- **Magic items.** Name when made inserts the base item's name and the bonus as values from two buttons (for example "Longsword +1"); no braces to type. In Try it, a magic weapon made for a group of weapons (such as any martial melee weapon) is tried on the first of them by name, held with its attack line and bonus, and a note names that weapon.
- **Armor.** An armor or shield whose "Armor Class as shown" is left empty shows its Armor Class in the equipment list (such as "15 + Dex (max 2)"), written from its Armor Class and type, and kept in step when you change them.
- **Creatures.** A stat block's spell list takes a spell by its name when the spell isn't installed, and each spell has a note ("level 5 version").
- **Damage that grows with level.** The rule has a **Dice below the table** setting (expert): the breath weapon's dice before the table's first level or with no table, so a breath weapon written that way can be made and copied. Its line among the character's rule notes rolls the same dice as its Breath Weapon attack.
- **Where used** counts the choices that offer an element by its level or school (such as "a 3rd-level evocation"), a class's "a spell from your list" choices, and the choices a 2014 element answered for its 2024 replacement. New elements you haven't saved yet are listed too, marked "Not saved yet", and open where you left them.
- **Search every pack.** The search box above your homebrew packs also finds the elements in all of them (by name, kind, tag or text). Choose one to see its pack, and open it from there.
- **The status line keeps your confirmations.** After you save an element it keeps "Saved 'Ember Bolt' to Ember Isles" and adds the element's problems when there are any ("· 2 problems"). The creator's other messages stay the same way, such as "Made a copy of 'Wizard' to edit", "Rule added." and "Feature added at level 2.", until you change the element. A screen reader hears each message once.
- **Ready for very large libraries.** With a big imported library (a whole 5etools-format library, dozens of Aurora-format packs), on phones too: saving an element prepares again only what changed and never holds a second copy of your library in memory, so the app is not closed for lack of memory on phones with less of it; searching catches up with a save at once; the checks of the element you are editing run one at a time instead of piling up in the background; and Try it opens in moments.

### Also in 1.5.0
- **Conditions on the Play tab.** The Conditions card lists the conditions under Exhaustion, each with a switch. A condition you switch on is marked Active, stays on through rests and is saved with the character, and its row shows its text. A condition made in the Homebrew Creator applies its rules while it is on (for example a lower speed or a penalty to ability checks); one made with **Listed as a condition** off is left out of the list, in both editions.
- **Monster entries show the whole stat block.** In the Compendium, a creature reads as a full stat block: size, type and alignment, Armor Class, hit points, initiative and speed, the six ability scores with their modifiers, saving throws, skills, damage and condition lines, senses with passive Perception, languages and challenge, then its traits, actions, and legendary and lair sections.
- **Weapon entries name their category and properties** ("Weapon category. Martial Melee"; "Two-Handed", not "Twohanded"), and a description's facts start on a line of their own.
- **iPhone: the keyboard no longer pushes the screen up.** With a text box focused the screen stays put, the keyboard covers only the bottom, and the box you type in stays in view.
- **Phones: an empty choice in a dropdown reads "None"** in the list sheet, instead of a blank row.
- **VoiceOver on iPhone reads more buttons as buttons,** such as the statistic explanations, Pick buttons and the dice roller's More button.
- **The Equipment inventory's commands use Aurora's colours,** light grey when they can be used and dim when they can't.
- **Screen readers follow the page's structure.** The character's name, build and experience beside the main tabs are a named area, and so are the quick actions beside the section tabs. The list headings on the Build tab (such as a choice group or Granted feats) sit one level below the page title, so heading navigation follows the page, and two choices of the same name read as "1 of 2" and "2 of 2". On phones, the creator's PREVIEW tab reads as one panel.
- **The text boxes of the New pack, New element and New character panels** show a thin highlighted border while you type in them.

### Fixed in 1.5.0
- **Repeatable 2024 invocations can be taken again.** A warlock can take Agonizing Blast, Eldritch Spear, Repelling Blast and Lessons of the First Ones more than once, as their Repeatable line says.
- **"Any language" choices no longer offer Druidic.** Secret languages such as Druidic and Thieves' Cant come only from the class that teaches them; a character who already chose one keeps it.
- **Android: Borealis no longer lists itself in its own share sheet,** where choosing it did nothing, and a file shared to Borealis that it can't open now says why.
- **A description's Source line names an imported book once:** "Player's Handbook (2024)", not "Player's Handbook (2024) (2024)".
- **The Situational list on the Play tab's Traits shows plain sentences** (#70), reported by Jack walljumper. Magic Initiate no longer adds rows of raw rule data there (its free cast is counted with your other uses, and its spell swap is in the feat's own text), and an advantage row is labelled "Saving throws", not "saving_throw". The same goes for other features that showed raw data there, such as the 2024 Rogue's and Monk's weapon training, Pact of the Blade, Spell Mastery, Signature Spells and Wild Companion.
- **macOS 26: the spell Selection slots table names each slot in full** ("Cantrip (Wizard 1) (1 of 3)") for screen readers, as it already did on newer macOS.
