**Borealis** 1.5.0 brings the Homebrew Creator: make your own spells, feats, classes, species, backgrounds, items, creatures and more inside Borealis, with forms and plain sentences, try them on a test character and share them as one file.

**Download for your platform at [borealisbuilder.com](https://borealisbuilder.com)**, or pick a file below.

| Platform | File |
|---|---|
| macOS (Apple silicon, macOS 10.15 or later) | `Borealis_1.5.0_aarch64.dmg` |
| Windows (x64) | `Borealis_1.5.0_x64-setup.exe` |
| Linux (x86_64), any distribution | `Borealis_1.5.0_amd64.AppImage` |
| Debian / Ubuntu | `Borealis_1.5.0_amd64.deb` |

Installing 1.5.0 over any earlier version keeps your characters, content and settings. On Windows, macOS and the Linux AppImage, **Install and restart** in the update notice installs it for you.

### New in 1.5.0: the Homebrew Creator
- **Start > Homebrew** holds your own packs. Make a pack with a name, an edition (2014, 2024 or both) and a version, and open it: the kinds of element with their counts, the element list, the editor and the element's live description, laid out like the Equipment page. What you save loads like any other content, so your characters can take it straight away.
- **Every kind of content.** Spells, feats, species and 2014 races with their subraces and traits, classes, subclasses, class options such as fighting styles, backgrounds, creatures (full stat blocks for companions, familiars and wild shapes), items, weapons, armor, magic items and starting equipment, and the smaller lists such as languages, conditions, skills and deities. **Other element** makes anything else, for experts.
- **Forms, not files.** Every field has its label and, where needed, a line of help. Anything that names other content is picked from a list, and conditions such as "only while wearing no armor" are built from choices, so there is never an id, a formula or an escaped character to type. Descriptions have bold, italic, headings, lists and tables.
- **Rules as plain sentences.** A rule gives something, lets the player choose, or changes a number, and ready-made rules cover common features: a pool of uses, extra damage, a sense, advantage on a save and many more.
- **Classes from level 1 to 20.** The Levels grid shows each level's features; add one with the + on its row. **Add spellcasting** gives a class its ability, spell list and slots (full, half, third, pact magic or your own table), and multiclassing and starting equipment have their own tabs.
- **Try it.** Test any element on a character built in memory: change the edition, level and ability scores, make its choices and read its hit points, Armor Class, skills, features and spell slots. The level ladder shows what is gained at every level from 1 to 20. The test character is never saved.
- **Problems as you type.** Borealis checks the element while you work and says what is wrong in plain words ("A spell needs a school."); click a problem to jump to its field. It also tries the element on a character at every level from 1 to 20 before you save.
- **Nothing is lost.** Every change is kept on your device as you type, through a crash or a restart. An element you changed is marked in the list and asks **Keep editing** or **Discard** when you open it again, and a new element you never saved stays listed as "Not saved yet". Undo and Redo (Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Shift+Z) cover every change.
- **Start from a copy** of a System Reference Document element or of anything installed, a whole class included. **Copy to homebrew** in the Compendium and the builder does the same, and **Edit** on installed content makes a copy, so updates never undo your changes. **Spell Lists** adds your spells to installed classes' lists.
- **Share a pack.** **Export** puts a pack in one file anyone with Borealis can import and edit, on any platform. Text you copied from content you imported is left out unless you choose otherwise. **Import** shows the usual preview first and lets you replace your pack (it asks first when characters on this device use something the file doesn't have) or add the file as a copy, and **Send** moves packs to your other devices on the same network.
- **For experts:** a read-only Data view of each element, Where used, multi-select edits (edition, tags, move, duplicate) undone in one step, and a keyboard shortcut for everything (**Keys** lists them).
- **On phones too.** The whole creator works on a phone: one screen at a time, Undo, Redo, Try it and Save pinned at the bottom, pickers and dialogs as sheets, the formatting buttons above the keyboard and tables edited cell by cell, bold and italic included.
- **A guide for it.** The help button in New pack and New element opens the new guide, [Make your own homebrew](https://borealisbuilder.com/guides/homebrew-creator).

### Also in 1.5.0
- **Monster entries show the whole stat block.** In the Compendium, a creature reads as a full stat block: size, type and alignment, Armor Class, hit points, initiative and speed, the six ability scores with their modifiers, saving throws, skills, damage and condition lines, senses with passive Perception, languages and challenge, then its traits, actions, and legendary and lair sections.
- **Weapon entries name their category and properties** ("Weapon category. Martial Melee"; "Two-Handed", not "Twohanded"), and a description's facts start on a line of their own.
- **iPhone: the keyboard no longer pushes the screen up.** With a text box focused the screen stays put, the keyboard covers only the bottom, and the box you type in stays in view.
- **Phones: an empty choice in a dropdown reads "None"** in the list sheet, instead of a blank row.
- **VoiceOver on iPhone reads more buttons as buttons,** such as the statistic explanations, Pick buttons and the dice roller's More button.
- **The Equipment inventory's commands use Aurora's colours,** light grey when they can be used and dim when they can't.

### Fixed in 1.5.0
- **Repeatable 2024 invocations can be taken again.** A warlock can take Agonizing Blast, Eldritch Spear, Repelling Blast and Lessons of the First Ones more than once, as their Repeatable line says.
- **"Any language" choices no longer offer Druidic.** Secret languages such as Druidic and Thieves' Cant come only from the class that teaches them; a character who already chose one keeps it.
- **Android: Borealis no longer lists itself in its own share sheet,** where choosing it did nothing, and a file shared to Borealis that it can't open now says why.
