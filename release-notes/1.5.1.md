**Borealis** 1.5.1 smooths the Homebrew Creator's forms: more suggestions from your installed content, fewer rules left as blocks, and warnings for the mistakes that are easy to miss.

**Download for your platform at [borealisbuilder.com](https://borealisbuilder.com)**, or pick a file below.

| Platform | File |
|---|---|
| macOS (Apple silicon, macOS 10.15 or later) | `Borealis_1.5.1_aarch64.dmg` |
| Windows (x64) | `Borealis_1.5.1_x64-setup.exe` |
| Linux (x86_64), any distribution | `Borealis_1.5.1_amd64.AppImage` |
| Debian / Ubuntu | `Borealis_1.5.1_amd64.deb` |
| Fedora / openSUSE | `Borealis-1.5.1-1.x86_64.rpm` |
| Android (64-bit ARM), sideload | `Borealis_1.5.1_arm64.apk` |

Installing 1.5.1 over any earlier version keeps your characters, content and settings. On Windows, macOS and the Linux AppImage, **Install and restart** in the update notice installs it for you.

### Homebrew Creator
- **Spell lists:** a spell's Lists also offers the lists of classes that write their spell list as a combination (such as "Cleric or Wizard"). A list that no installed class or choice uses gets a warning that names the near spelling ("there is a list Wizard"), and a spell on no list gets a hint.
- **Spellcasting:** the Spell list of a spellcasting section suggests the lists your installed classes have, and still takes a new one.
- **Suggestions from your content:** an item's Category offers the categories your installed items use, and Tags suggests the tags installed elements of the same kind carry.
- **Magic items:** Name when made inserts the base item's name and the bonus as values from two buttons (for example "Longsword +1"); no braces to type.
- **2014 races:** a Subraces field says whether the player chooses one of the race's subraces, instead of a rule block.
- **Classes:** **Insert class table** in the description adds the class table from the Levels grid: level, proficiency bonus, features and the class's own columns.
- **Class options:** a warning when no installed class's choice offers the option's group, with the near spelling when there is one.
- **Backgrounds:** variants that a background offers by tag (as many imported backgrounds do) are listed in its Variants field, and the origin feat picker offers origin feats only.
- **Creatures:** a stat block's spell list takes a spell by its name when the spell isn't installed, and each spell has a note ("level 5 version").
- **Copies:** a class copied with its subclasses names them in the editor ("Saved with it: ...") before you save.
- **Where used:** counts the choices that offer an element by its level or school (such as "a 3rd-level evocation"), a class's "a spell from your list" choices, and the choices a 2014 element answered for its 2024 replacement. New elements you haven't saved yet are listed too, marked "Not saved yet", and open where you left them.
- **Search every pack:** the search box above your homebrew packs also finds the elements in all of them (by name, kind, tag or text). Choose one to see its pack, and open it from there.
- **Skills:** a skill you make is a real skill on every character: its own row with its ability's modifier and passive score, on the sheet and the PDF. A background, a class's skill choice or any "choose a skill" can give proficiency in it, and expertise works as for the others.
- **Spell lists on phones:** **Select** chooses spells with a tap for Add selected and Remove selected, where a desktop uses Ctrl+click and Shift+click.
- **Armor:** an armor or shield whose "Armor Class as shown" is left empty shows its Armor Class in the equipment list (such as "15 + Dex (max 2)"), written from its Armor Class and type, and kept in step when you change them.
- **Try it:** a magic weapon made for a group of weapons (such as any martial melee weapon) is tried on the first of them by name, held with its attack line and bonus, and a note names that weapon.
- **Feats:** a prerequisite on a statistic reads in words, such as "A flying speed" or "Darkvision range 60+".
- **Rules:** the "Damage that grows with level" rule has a **Dice below the table** setting (expert), the breath weapon's dice before the table's first level or with no table, so a breath weapon written that way can be made and copied. Its line among the character's rule notes rolls the same dice as its Breath Weapon attack.

### Play
- **Conditions:** the Conditions card on the Play tab lists the conditions under Exhaustion, each with a switch. A condition you switch on is marked Active, stays on through rests and is saved with the character, and its row shows its text. A condition made in the Homebrew Creator applies its rules while it is on (for example a lower speed or a penalty to ability checks). One made with **Listed as a condition** off is left out of the list, in both editions.

### Accessibility
- The text boxes of the New pack, New element and New character panels show a thin highlighted border while you type in them.
- Screen readers: the quick actions beside the section tabs are inside a named area, two choices of the same name read as "1 of 2" and "2 of 2", and the creator's PREVIEW tab on phones no longer reports a nested side panel.
