- **Deities**: the SRD 5.1 pantheon tables (Appendix PH-B) are not emitted as `Deity` elements; the table columns do not extract reliably.
- **Monsters, familiars and companions**: SRD monster stat blocks are not part of the character-builder content (Find Familiar, Wild Shape forms and similar spell/feature riders are text only).
- **Human variant, race names, height/weight**: not in the SRD 5.1, so not authored.
- **Rule coverage**: class numeric tables, proficiencies, saves, AC formulas, speed, spell slots, spells known/prepared, cantrips, expertise and ability score changes are encoded as rules. Many purely tactical features (reactions, once-per-rest riders, damage riders, spell-like invocation uses) are descriptive text with a sheet summary only. Magic items carry rules only where the effect is a plain statistic (enhancement bonuses, AC/save bonuses, ability score set items, resistances).
- **Spell scrolls, class ability-score/feat features, multiclass elements, proxy items**: generated by the loader (content-format section 10), not stored.
- **Equipment tables**: food, drink, lodging and services tables are not emitted as items; the Burglar's Pack has no extract entry for its ball-bearing bag (not listed as an item in the SRD gear table).
- **Magic item tables**: table rows inside item text are kept as plain text lines.
